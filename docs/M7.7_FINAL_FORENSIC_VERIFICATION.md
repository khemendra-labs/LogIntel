## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone M7.7 introduces deterministic, evidence-grounded case comparison, campaign correlation candidate generation, and cross-investigation analysis capabilities to the LogIntel investigation platform. M7.7 enables forensic analysts to compare two or more active or closed investigations (bounded 2 to 5 cases) and discover structured, verifiable overlaps across multiple analytical dimensions without automated or speculative attribution.

The system enforces the strict trust hierarchy and analytical separation:

$$\text{CASE A} + \text{CASE B} \longrightarrow \text{DETERMINISTIC COMPARISON} \longrightarrow \text{SHARED PATTERNS} \longrightarrow \text{CAMPAIGN CORRELATION CANDIDATES} \longrightarrow \text{ANALYST REVIEW}$$

Crucially, M7.7 maintains the non-negotiable forensic invariant:

$$\text{CORRELATION} \neq \text{PROOF} \neq \text{ATTRIBUTION} \neq \text{MALICIOUSNESS} \neq \text{CONFIDENCE SCORE} \neq \text{PROBABILITY} \neq \text{DETECTION}$$

Key Architectural Properties Verified:
1. **Zero Numerical Probability / Confidence:** Every correlation candidate expresses categorical basis dimensions (`ENTITY_OVERLAP`, `TEMPORAL_OVERLAP`, `TECHNIQUE_OVERLAP`, etc.) with zero floating-point confidence, probability, or risk scores.
2. **Authoritative Evidence Immutability:** Authoritative forensic telemetry in `logintel.db` remained strictly untouched and read-only.
3. **No Evidence Duplication:** Comparison records reference cases, entities, findings, and events by identifier and hash; no raw telemetry rows are copied.
4. **Epistemic Model Preservation:** Distinct epistemic statuses (`OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`) are strictly separated from analyst review states (`UNREVIEWED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `CORROBORATED`, `WEAKENED`, `DISPUTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `REJECTED`). Shared characteristics do not upgrade inference to observation.
5. **Absence of Telemetry Preserved:** Missing data is explicitly categorized (`NO_EVENT_OBSERVED`, `TELEMETRY_DROPPED`, `RULE_NOT_CONFIGURED`) and never converted into negative proof of absence.
6. **Blake2b Cryptographic Provenance:** Every comparison result produces a canonical provenance manifest hashing case IDs, shared entities, and correlation candidate IDs.
7. **Defanged Multi-Format Export:** Comparison reports export to byte-deterministic JSON, CSV (formula injection defanged with single-quote escaping), and Markdown.
8. **Strict Case Isolation & Authorization:** All comparison operations require valid bearer authentication and explicit case-level access validation.

## 6. Case Comparison Architecture

Case comparison operates on explicit, bounded sets of cases:
1. **Primary Case Identification:** Root case providing authorization context.
2. **Comparison Target Selection:** 1 to 4 additional target cases (enforcing a hard cap of 5 cases maximum to prevent denial-of-service).
3. **Multi-Case Scope Bounds:** Configurable dimensions:
   - Entities (`HOST`, `USER`, `IP`, `PROCESS`, `COMMAND`, `FILE`, `SESSION`, etc.)
   - Timeline events and absolute/relative temporal boundaries
   - Analyst findings and epistemic states
   - Investigation hypotheses and supporting/contradicting evidence
   - Governed threat hunts and query results
   - MITRE ATT&CK technique classifications
   - Evidence gaps and dropped telemetry states
4. **Isolated Extraction:** Each case's telemetry is independently retrieved and canonicalized prior to intersection; no cross-case leaking occurs.

## 7. Campaign Correlation Architecture

Campaign correlation produces derived candidate objects (`CampaignCorrelationCandidate`):
- `candidate_id`: Deterministic UUIDv4 identifier.
- `case_ids`: Explicit list of involved cases.
- `correlation_type`: Categorical discriminator (`SHARED_ENTITY`, `SHARED_TECHNIQUE`, `SHARED_TIMELINE_PATTERN`, etc.).
- `correlation_basis`: Analytical foundation (`ENTITY_OVERLAP`, `TECHNIQUE_OVERLAP`, `PROCESS_CONTINUITY`, etc.).
- `corroboration_nature`: Evidentiary relation (`CORROBORATING`, `CONTRADICTING`, `CONTEXTUAL`, `TEMPORAL`, `ENTITY_LINK`, `UNKNOWN`).
- `shared_entities`: Explicit list of overlapping entity names and types.
- `source_references`: Cryptographic cross-references to underlying case evidence.
- `epistemic_status`: Preserved epistemic boundary (`OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`).
- `analyst_review_status`: Explicit workflow state (`UNREVIEWED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `CORROBORATED`, `WEAKENED`, `DISPUTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `REJECTED`).
- `analyst_notes`: Traceable analyst justification for review transitions.

## 8. Correlation Semantics & Epistemic Boundaries

M7.7 preserves the epistemic distinction between what was directly captured in authoritative logs and what is inferred by analysis:

```text
[OBSERVED TELEMETRY] ──(immutable)──> Cannot be altered by comparison
[SHARED ENTITY]      ──(deterministic overlap)──> Observation that two cases share an IP/host
[ATTRIBUTION]        ──(prohibited automation)──> Never asserted automatically
```

1. **Non-Upgrading Rule:** If Case A has `INFERRED` lateral movement and Case B has `OBSERVED` lateral movement, the comparison candidate is marked `INFERRED`. An inference is never upgraded to `OBSERVED` merely because another case observed it.
2. **Absence of Telemetry:** If Case A has process execution records and Case B has no execution logs, the system records `NO_EXECUTION_TELEMETRY_OBSERVED` under evidence gaps. It never asserts "Case B had no execution."
3. **No Numerical Probabilities:** Numerical confidence scores are prohibited. Overlap is strictly categorical.

## 9. Evidence and Provenance

Every case comparison artifact includes a Blake2b cryptographic provenance manifest:
- **Hash Algorithm:** `BLAKE2b-256`
- **Canonical Serialization:** Deterministic JSON with sorted keys and UTF-8 encoding.
- **Manifest Fields:**
  - `manifest_id`: UUID
  - `comparison_id`: Referenced comparison
  - `case_ids`: Canonical sorted list of evaluated case IDs
  - `entities_hash`: Blake2b digest of all shared entity strings
  - `candidates_hash`: Blake2b digest of all correlation candidate IDs
  - `overall_manifest_digest`: Root Blake2b digest sealing the entire comparison
  - `generated_at`: ISO 8601 UTC timestamp

Provenance manifests are independently verifiable and prevent tampering across all export formats.

## 11. Case Isolation Verification

Cross-case authorization boundaries were strictly tested:
1. When comparing Case A and Case B, the calling user must be authorized for Case A. Attempting to query Case A's comparison endpoint with a token possessing no authorization for Case A fails immediately with `403 Forbidden` or `404 Not Found`.
2. Telemetry extraction is parameter-bound to specific `case_id` values. No comparison operation permits un-scoped SQL queries or wildcard queries across other cases in `cases.db`.
3. Cached comparison results are stored with explicit primary `case_id` ownership, preventing cross-tenant leakage.
