## 7. Epistemic Truth Model

LogIntel strictly preserves three distinct epistemic ground-truth states:
- **`OBSERVED`**: Direct forensic facts established by operating system telemetry.
- **`INFERRED`**: Analytical conclusions derived from correlation, temporal reconstruction, or behavioral heuristics.
- **`UNKNOWN`**: Explicitly identified telemetry gaps or unmonitored phases.

**The Epistemic Invariant:** Analyst disposition never alters forensic ground truth:
```text
OBSERVED + ACCEPTED  → OBSERVED (accepted by analyst)
INFERRED + ACCEPTED  → INFERRED (analyst concurred; forensic reality remains inferred)
UNKNOWN  + ACCEPTED  → UNKNOWN  (acknowledged gap; forensic reality remains unknown)
INFERRED + REJECTED  → INFERRED (analyst rejected analytical inference)
UNKNOWN  + DISPUTED  → UNKNOWN  (analyst disputes gap assessment)
```

---

## 8. Analyst Review Model

Analyst reviews represent human decisions recorded in `cases.db`:
- `UNREVIEWED`: Initial state upon automated synthesis.
- `ACCEPTED`: Analyst agrees with finding relevance.
- `REJECTED`: Analyst rejects finding as false positive or irrelevant.
- `DISPUTED`: Analyst flags finding for additional evidentiary corroboration.

All review state changes generate immutable audit log entries in `case_audit_log` with timestamp, actor attribution, and justification notes.
