## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Milestone M7.8 establishes a deterministic, auditable, and mathematically grounded **Investigation Quality, Closure & Forensic Review** layer for LogIntel.

The system enforces the strict forensic trust invariant:
$$\text{AUTHORITATIVE EVIDENCE} \neq \text{ANALYST FINDING} \neq \text{HYPOTHESIS} \neq \text{ASSESSMENT} \neq \text{REVIEW STATE} \neq \text{CLOSURE READINESS} \neq \text{REPORT NARRATIVE} \neq \text{AI ADVISORY}$$

Key Architectural Properties Verified:
1. **Deterministic Multi-Gate Review Engine:** Evaluates investigations across 12 distinct analytical gates (`SCOPE`, `EVIDENCE_COVERAGE`, `FINDING_GROUNDING`, `HYPOTHESIS_REVIEW`, `CONTRADICTIONS`, `QUESTIONS`, `HUNTS`, `TIMELINE_CONSISTENCY`, `GRAPH_CONSISTENCY`, `REPORT_CONSISTENCY`, `PACKAGE_CONSISTENCY`, and `HANDOFF`).
2. **Deterministic Categorical Closure Blockers:** Identifies and classifies blockers into categorical severities (`BLOCKER`, `WARNING`, `INFO`) with a governed analyst waiver/acknowledgment audit trail.
3. **Categorical Closure Readiness State Machine:** Derives case closure readiness (`NOT_READY`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, `READY_FOR_CLOSURE`, `CLOSED`, `REOPENED`) purely through deterministic rule evaluation. No numerical confidence, probabilities, risk scores, or autonomous AI decisions are permitted.
4. **Governed Closure & Controlled Reopening:** Formal closure transitions require explicit authenticated analyst authorization, validation of zero unresolved mandatory blockers, and append-only immutable audit logging. Reopening preserves historical review snapshots without mutating authoritative telemetry.
5. **Blake2b Cryptographic Provenance Manifests:** Every review snapshot is sealed with a root Blake2b cryptographic digest over canonical JSON representations of gate results, blockers, and coverage metrics.
6. **Multi-Format Deterministic Export:** Supports canonical JSON, formula-injection-safe CSV (formula defanging with `'`), and structured Markdown exports.
7. **Advisory-Only Local AI Summary:** Provides advisory summaries and recommendations with strict prompt injection containment, explicit attribution (`is_authoritative = False`, `advisory_only = True`), and zero automated state transitions.
8. **Zero Schema Migrations:** Schema migrations remain strictly frozen at **5** (`logintel.db` remains 100% read-only; all case review states utilize existing `cases.db` structures).

## 2. MISSION

The mission of M7.8 is to provide an objective forensic review system that answers:
1. *Is the investigation sufficiently scoped?*
2. *Are the relevant evidence sources represented?*
3. *Are important telemetry gaps explicitly documented?*

10. *Is the case ready for closure?*
11. *If not, what deterministic review blockers remain?*
12. *Can the case be closed without silently converting uncertainty into certainty?*

## 9. REVIEW ENGINE

The review engine evaluates case state in a strictly deterministic sequence:
1. **Scope Gate:** Validates investigation objective, scope entity targets, and subject identifiers.
2. **Evidence Coverage Gate:** Reconciles referenced evidence against observable sources and identifies dropped telemetry.
3. **Finding Grounding Gate:** Categorizes findings as `GROUNDED`, `PARTIALLY_GROUNDED`, or `UNRESOLVED` based on evidence references.
4. **Hypothesis Gate:** Inspects supporting vs. contradicting evidence counts and flags unreviewed hypotheses.
5. **Contradiction Gate:** Identifies unresolved contradictions across findings and hypotheses without automated mutation.
6. **Question Gate:** Categorizes open questions into `ANSWERED`, `PARTIALLY_ANSWERED`, `UNANSWERED`, `BLOCKED_BY_TELEMETRY`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
7. **Hunt Gate:** Validates governed threat hunt executions and ensures candidate findings remain unpromoted without analyst action.
8. **Timeline & Graph Gates:** Validates referential alignment across timeline items and attack graph entities.
9. **Report & Package Gates:** Flags stale reports or unindexed artifacts.
10. **Handoff Gate:** Reconciles pending follow-up obligations.

Canonical ordering is strictly enforced across all gate evaluation outputs:
$$\text{blocker\_id ASC} \implies \text{gate\_type ASC} \implies \text{category ASC} \implies \text{source\_id ASC}$$

## 10. CLOSURE READINESS MODEL

Closure readiness is derived from deterministic rules:
- If case status is `CLOSED` $\implies$ `CLOSED`.
- If unresolved blockers with severity `BLOCKER` exist $\implies$ `NOT_READY`.
- If unresolved blockers with severity `WARNING` exist $\implies$ `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
- If unacknowledged `INFO` items exist $\implies$ `READY_FOR_REVIEW`.
- If zero unresolved mandatory blockers or warnings exist $\implies$ `READY_FOR_CLOSURE`.

Closure readiness is strictly an **analytical workflow state**, never a probability or truth score.
