## 7. Epistemic Truth Model

LogIntel strictly preserves three distinct epistemic states:
- **`OBSERVED`**: Factually recorded in system telemetry.
- **`INFERRED`**: Analytically deduced from correlation or behavioral sequence.
- **`UNKNOWN`**: Missing telemetry or unobserved attack phase.

**Non-Negotiable Invariant:** Acceptance by an analyst does NOT transform an inference into observed fact:
```text
OBSERVED + ACCEPTED  → OBSERVED (accepted)
INFERRED + ACCEPTED  → INFERRED (analyst concurred; forensic truth unchanged)
UNKNOWN  + ACCEPTED  → UNKNOWN  (acknowledged gap; forensic truth unchanged)
INFERRED + REJECTED  → INFERRED (analyst rejected inference)
UNKNOWN  + DISPUTED  → UNKNOWN  (analyst disputes gap assessment)
```

---

## 8. Analyst Review Model

Analyst review states are recorded in `cases.db` to track human decision-making:
- `UNREVIEWED`: Initial state upon automated synthesis.
- `ACCEPTED`: Analyst agrees with finding relevance.
- `REJECTED`: Analyst deems finding irrelevant or false positive.
- `DISPUTED`: Analyst questions finding validity or evidence sufficiency.

Review state transitions trigger append-only audit records in `case_audit_log` with actor attribution, timestamp, and review commentary.
