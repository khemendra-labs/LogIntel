## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mission

Milestone M7.8 strengthens the quality-control, consistency, and closure layer of the LogIntel investigation platform. It provides deterministic, evidence-grounded review gates that enable analysts, lead investigators, and reviewers to evaluate an investigation across 12 deterministic gates prior to closure:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{CASE STATE} &+ \text{EVIDENCE COVERAGE} + \text{ANALYTICAL CONSISTENCY} + \text{REVIEW COMPLETENESS} \\
&+ \text{OPEN QUESTIONS} + \text{TELEMETRY GAPS} + \text{PROVENANCE INTEGRITY} \\
&\longrightarrow \text{DETERMINISTIC FORENSIC REVIEW} \longrightarrow \text{CLOSURE READINESS}
\end{aligned}$$

Crucially, M7.8 enforces the platform's core epistemic invariant:

$$\text{AUTHORITATIVE EVIDENCE} \neq \text{ANALYST FINDING} \neq \text{HYPOTHESIS} \neq \text{ASSESSMENT} \neq \text{REVIEW STATE} \neq \text{CLOSURE READINESS} \neq \text{REPORT NARRATIVE} \neq \text{AI ADVISORY}$$

The system strictly avoids converting:
- review completeness $\rightarrow$ evidence truth
- investigation completeness $\rightarrow$ maliciousness
- closure readiness $\rightarrow$ incident confirmation
- analyst approval $\rightarrow$ authoritative evidence
- absence of evidence $\rightarrow$ evidence of absence

Closure readiness is an **analytical workflow state**, never a probability, numerical confidence score, or truth assertion.

## 6. Architecture Design for M7.8

### 6.1 Domain Models (`apps/engine/src/logintel/review/models.py`)
- `ReviewGateType`: Enum for the 12 review gates (`SCOPE`, `EVIDENCE_COVERAGE`, `FINDING_GROUNDING`, `HYPOTHESIS_REVIEW`, `CONTRADICTIONS`, `QUESTIONS`, `HUNTS`, `TIMELINE_CONSISTENCY`, `GRAPH_CONSISTENCY`, `REPORT_CONSISTENCY`, `PACKAGE_CONSISTENCY`, `HANDOFF`).
- `GateEvaluationStatus`: `PASS`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
- `ReviewBlockerCategory`: `MISSING_SCOPE`, `UNRESOLVED_EVIDENCE_REFERENCE`, `UNREVIEWED_FINDING`, `UNREVIEWED_HYPOTHESIS`, `OPEN_INVESTIGATION_QUESTION`, `UNRESOLVED_CONTRADICTION`, `TELEMETRY_GAP_REQUIRES_REVIEW`, `STALE_REPORT`, `PACKAGE_INCONSISTENCY`, `HANDOFF_FOLLOWUP_REQUIRED`, `PROVENANCE_REFERENCE_FAILURE`.
- `BlockerSeverity`: `BLOCKER`, `WARNING`, `INFO`.
- `BlockerResolutionState`: `UNRESOLVED`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `RESOLVED`, `WAIVED`.
- `ClosureReadinessState`: `NOT_READY`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, `READY_FOR_CLOSURE`, `CLOSED`, `REOPENED`.
- `FindingGroundingStatus`: `GROUNDED`, `PARTIALLY_GROUNDED`, `UNRESOLVED`.
- `QuestionReviewStatus`: `ANSWERED`, `PARTIALLY_ANSWERED`, `UNANSWERED`, `BLOCKED_BY_TELEMETRY`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
- `ReviewBlocker`: Structured model representing a blocker.
- `ReviewGateResult`: Structured evaluation of a specific gate with findings, blockers, and recommendations.
- `CaseReviewSnapshot`: Complete review artifact containing all gate evaluations, blockers, coverage metrics, closure readiness, and Blake2b provenance manifest.

### 6.2 Service Architecture (`apps/engine/src/logintel/review/service.py`)
- `InvestigationReviewService`:
  - `run_forensic_review(case_id, actor)`: Deterministically executes all 12 review gates, aggregates blockers, derives closure readiness, computes Blake2b provenance digest, persists review snapshot in `case_assessments`, and logs audit entry.
  - `acknowledge_blocker(case_id, blocker_id, resolution_state, notes, actor)`: Allows an analyst to acknowledge or waive a blocker with mandatory audit trail.
  - `close_case(case_id, closure_notes, actor)`: Enforces that no unacknowledged/unresolved `BLOCKER` severities exist; updates case status to `CLOSED`, increments version, and logs audit record.
  - `reopen_case(case_id, reopen_reason, actor)`: Authorizes transitioning case from `CLOSED` to `ACTIVE`, increments version, and logs audit record.
  - `export_review(case_id, format, actor)`: Deterministically formats review snapshot to JSON, defanged CSV, or Markdown.
  - `generate_ai_review_summary(case_id, actor)`: Advisory-only loopback AI summary with prompt injection containment and `is_authoritative = False`.

### 6.3 API Surface (`/api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/`)
- `GET /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review`: Retrieve current review snapshot.
- `POST /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/run`: Execute on-demand forensic review across all 12 gates.
- `GET /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/blockers`: Retrieve current blockers with filtering.
- `POST /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/blockers/{blocker_id}/acknowledge`: Acknowledge/waive a blocker.
- `GET /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/history`: Retrieve review lifecycle audit history.
- `POST /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/close`: Explicitly authorize case closure.
- `POST /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/reopen`: Explicitly authorize case reopening.
- `POST /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/ai-summary`: Advisory-only AI summary generation.
- `GET /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/review/export`: Export review in JSON, CSV, or Markdown.
