## 7. Episode Reconstruction

Episodes group evidence chronologically by functional activity:
- `AUTHENTICATION_BURST`: SSH or local login attempts within temporal proximity.
- `PRIVILEGE_CHANGE_EPISODE`: Sudo, root escalation, or permission changes.
- `LATERAL_MOVEMENT_EPISODE`: Cross-host network flows and remote executions.
- `NETWORK_ACTIVITY_EPISODE`: Inbound/outbound traffic bursts.
- `PERSISTENCE_EPISODE`: Scheduled tasks, service creations, or cron modifications.
- `PROCESS_EXECUTION_EPISODE`: Command line and child process executions.

Episodes are bounded to maximum 50 episodes per reconstruction.

---

## 8. Transition Intelligence

Transitions detect deterministic steps between consecutive events, episodes, or graph nodes:
- `USER_TO_HOST`: User authenticated to a host.
- `HOST_TO_HOST`: Activity hopped between hosts (lateral movement).
- `USER_TO_PROCESS`: Authenticated user invoked process.
- `PROCESS_TO_NETWORK_DESTINATION`: Process connected to external/remote IP.
- `STAGE_PROGRESSION`: Chronological step progression across episodes.

Each transition records:
- `from_entity`, `to_entity`, `timestamp`, `reason`, `correlation_basis`, `evidence_references`, `epistemic_status`, and `review_state`.

---

## 9. Temporal Evidence Chains

Explicit traceable chains connect source events to analytical conclusions:
$$\text{Event A} \longrightarrow \text{Entity} \longrightarrow \text{Transition} \longrightarrow \text{Event B}$$
Every step in `evidence_chains` contains:
- `step_index`, `source_record_type`, `source_record_id`, `citation_tag`, `timestamp`, `entity_key`, `action_or_relation`, and `epistemic_status`.

---

## 10. Evidence Gaps

Identifies visibility limitations without assuming negative proof:
- Types: `NO_TELEMETRY`, `MISSING_HOST_VISIBILITY`, `MISSING_PROCESS_TELEMETRY`, `MISSING_NETWORK_TELEMETRY`, `TIMESTAMP_GAP`, `UNRESOLVED_TRANSITION`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
- Principle: Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence ($\text{No observation} \neq \text{Non-occurrence}$).
- Each gap includes actionable remedies (`remedy`).

---

## 11. Multi-Host Reconstruction

Reconstructs cross-host lateral traces (`MultiHostTrace`):
- Tracks hops across hosts (e.g. `host-alpha` $\rightarrow$ `host-beta`).
- Preserves actor identity (`deployer`), hop count, transition lineage, and source evidence citations.

---

## 12. Incident/Campaign Correlation

Identifies potentially related incidents across the organization:
- Reasons: `SHARED_ENTITY`, `SHARED_ACCOUNT`, `SHARED_SOURCE`, `SHARED_DESTINATION`, `TEMPORAL_PROXIMITY`, `COMMON_SEQUENCE`.
- Statuses: `POTENTIALLY_RELATED`, `CORRELATED`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `UNRELATED`.
- Guarantees:
  - Zero numeric confidence scores or probabilities.
  - Zero arbitrary risk percentages.
  - Strictly evidence-based categorical relations.
