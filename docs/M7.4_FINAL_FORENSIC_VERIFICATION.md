## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone M7.4 delivers the dedicated analyst reasoning workbench for Program M7:
- Analysts can construct, version, review, and evaluate **findings** directly grounded in evidence references without modifying authoritative forensic telemetry.
- Strict epistemic separation is enforced: objective truth (`OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`) is decoupled from human analyst review (`UNREVIEWED`, `ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED`, `DISPUTED`). Reviewing an inferred finding as `ACCEPTED` never converts it into `OBSERVED`.
- Multiple competing **hypotheses** can be formulated and evaluated side-by-side using categorical supporting evidence, contradicting evidence, and explicit missing telemetry gaps.
- The forensic trust invariant is maintained:
  ```text
  OBSERVED EVIDENCE  ≠  ANALYST FINDING  ≠  HYPOTHESIS  ≠  FINAL CONCLUSION
  ```
- Migrations 7, 8, and 9 were **rejected**. Migrations remain frozen at 5.
- Disassociating evidence from a finding or deleting a finding/hypothesis never deletes underlying evidence references or canonical telemetry.

## 10. Findings & Hypothesis Semantics

### 10.1 Finding Model & Immutability
A finding is an analyst-authored analytical statement:
```text
Finding  ≠  Evidence
```
- A finding references evidence records without modifying them.
- Deleting a finding never deletes underlying evidence.
- An unsupported finding is strictly classified as `UNKNOWN` / uncorroborated.

### 10.2 Hypothesis Status Semantics (Non-Authoritative Assessment States)
```python
class HypothesisStatusM74(str, Enum):
    OPEN = "OPEN"
    SUPPORTED = "SUPPORTED"
    WEAKENED = "WEAKENED"
    DISPUTED = "DISPUTED"
    INCONCLUSIVE = "INCONCLUSIVE"
    REJECTED = "REJECTED"
```
- `OPEN`: Proposition under active investigation.
- `SUPPORTED`: Analyst-reviewed evidence currently supports the hypothesis within the investigation context. *Explicitly NOT: probability, confidence score, proof, authoritative fact, or automatic truth determination.*
- `WEAKENED`: Current evidence reduces analytical support for the hypothesis. *Explicitly NOT: numerical probability reduction.*
- `DISPUTED`: Analyst notes conflicting evidence or contradictory interpretations.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: Current evidence is insufficient to evaluate the proposition.
- `REJECTED`: Analyst-reviewed assessment that current evidence does not support the hypothesis. *Explicitly NOT: proof that the underlying event did not occur.*

### 10.3 Epistemic State Invariant
Objective epistemic state (`OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`) remains strictly independent from review status (`UNREVIEWED`, `ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED`, `DISPUTED`) and hypothesis status. Analyst review never converts an inference into an observed telemetry fact.
